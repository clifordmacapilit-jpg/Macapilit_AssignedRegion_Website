Activities & Experiences

Things to do across Northern Mindanao

Feel the Splash

🏖

Adventure

  • White water rafting on the Cagayan de Oro River
  • Hiking Mt. Hibok-Hibok
  • Trekking in Mt. Kitanglad Range
  • Swimming at Tinago Falls
🌿

Nature

  • Bird watching at Mapawa Nature Park
  • Island hopping and sandbar visits at White Island
  • Snorkeling at the Sunken Cemetery
  • Viewing Maria Cristina Falls
🎭

Culture

  • Join Camiguin's Lanzones Festival
  • Visit the old churches of Camiguin and Cagayan de Oro
  • Meet the Higaonon people of Bukidnon
  • Shop for local handicrafts
🍲

Food

  • Taste Camiguin's sweet lanzones and pastel buns
  • Try pineapples from Bukidnon
  • Eat satti, a skewered meat dish with spicy sauce
  • Sample Cagayan de Oro's street food
