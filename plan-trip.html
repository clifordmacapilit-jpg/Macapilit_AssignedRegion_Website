Travel Tips

  • Visit between December and May for drier weather.
  • Book rafting and ferry tickets early on weekends.
  • Bring cash since many small places do not take cards.
  • Respect Indigenous communities and ask before taking photos.

Packing Checklist

  • ☐ Swimwear and quick-dry clothes
  • ☐ Sunscreen and hat
  • ☐ Water shoes
  • ☐ Light rain jacket
  • ☐ Power bank and waterproof phone case
