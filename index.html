Welcome to Region 10

Northern Mindanao, or Region 10, covers the provinces of Bukidnon, Camiguin, Lanao del Norte and Misamis Occidental and Misamis Oriental. Its regional center is Cagayan de Oro City. It is known as the "Land of Promise" for its fertile highlands and busy ports.

Travelers come for the "City of Golden Friendship," the island of Camiguin, and the cool plateaus of Bukidnon. Whatever you like, there is something to do here.
