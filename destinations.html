Popular Destinations

Eight places you should not miss in Region 10

White Island

A crescent sandbar with no trees, floating off the coast of Camiguin. It offers sweeping views of Mt. Hibok-Hibok and clear shallow water for swimming and sunsets.

📍 Camiguin

Sunken Cemetery

A large cross marks the old cemetery that sank into the sea after the 1871 volcanic eruption. Snorkelers and kayakers visit it today, and it is a quiet memorial.

📍 Catarman, Camiguin

Mt. Hibok-Hibok

An active volcano that rises above the island. Hikers climb through forest trails to crater views and to the hot and cold springs below.

📍 Mambajao, Camiguin

Maria Cristina Falls

Known as the Twin Falls, it drops into a gorge on the Agus River. Its power feeds a hydroelectric plant that helps light much of Mindanao.

📍 Iligan City, Lanao del Norte

Tinago Falls

"Tinago" means hidden, and the falls sit at the bottom of a steep stairway. The turquoise pool is good for swimming under the cascade.

📍 Iligan City, Lanao del Norte

Mount Kitanglad Range

A protected natural park and one of the Philippines' highest mountain ranges. It is home to the Philippine eagle, wild orchids and cool highland air.

📍 Bukidnon

Cagayan de Oro River

The river is the country's white water rafting capital. Guides lead trips through rapids that suit beginners and thrill seekers.

📍 Cagayan de Oro, Misamis Oriental

Mapawa Nature Park

A forest park above the city with trails, a canopy walk and picnic areas. It is a cool, green escape for families and bird watchers.

📍 Cagayan de Oro, Misamis Oriental
